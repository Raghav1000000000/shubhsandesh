Show **GST-inclusive** prices. "Less than ₹3 a day" is the easy line for Regular.

## GST

- Software as a service is taxed at **18%**.
- GST registration becomes compulsory above **₹20 lakh** turnover a year, which is about 2,000 Regular customers.
- Below that, registration is optional. Registering early lets you claim input tax credit on tools and ads, and some business customers (CAs especially) will want a GST invoice.
- Confirm the details with a CA before launch.
